Low-resolution pixel art map for a top-down 3/4 view RPG, exactly 16 pixels per map tile, a grid of 32 by 24 tiles filling the whole frame edge to edge with no border.
The first reference image is the layout at the final size: trace over it exactly. Brown squares are small objects drawn on that spot. Dark green areas are blocked and must be filled with buildings, market stalls, walls, hedges or trees, never with sand. Do not add, widen, move or remove any path. The second reference image is only a small sample of the pixel style of the same world (colours, 1px outlines, tree and roof shapes); it is not a layout, do not copy its buildings or paths.

Low-resolution pixel art map for a top-down 3/4 view RPG, exactly 16 pixels per map tile, a grid of 32 by 24 tiles filling the whole frame edge to edge with no border.
The first reference image is the layout at the final size: trace over it exactly. Sand-coloured areas are walkable paths and must stay plain flat sand with exactly the same outline; white squares (where people will stand) and grey squares are plain sand path too. Brown squares are small objects drawn on that spot. Dark green areas are blocked and must be filled with buildings, walls, hedges or trees, never with sand. Do not add, widen, move or remove any path, and draw nothing on the paths. The bottom road ends at the trees: below it there is only forest.
The second reference image is only a small sample of the pixel style of the same world (colours, 1px outlines, tree and roof shapes); it is not a layout, do not copy its buildings or paths.
Place: the north end of the same busy market town at bright noon, cheerful and colourful. A wide street runs across the middle. At the top middle a walled square courtyard in front of a round-topped stone archway gate in a stone town wall; the single path tile at the very top passes through the gate opening and leaves the map at the top edge. A narrow 2-tile lane joins the courtyard down to the street. On the left, a chestnut shop yard (the brown square is sacks of chestnuts) with a narrow back alley above it. On the right, a flower lane (one brown square is a faded, washed-out grey striped awning stall, the other a small bundle of hair ribbons). Lower left and lower right yards: a flower cart, a little box of bells, a hand cart and a small bundle of hand mirrors on the brown squares. The blocked areas are market shop buildings with rounded terracotta and slate-blue tile roofs, low stone walls and round trees. A few small props (crates, barrels, flower pots) only on blocked green areas.
Style: crisp hard-edged pixels with no blur and no anti-aliasing, limited palette, 1px darker-hue outlines on objects and buildings (never black), flat ground shading with 2-3 shades, soft cel shading, child-friendly, light from the upper left. Paths are plain flat bright sand, calm, with clean edges.
Roofs stay rounded and smooth: rounded terracotta and slate-blue tile roofs in neat rows of tiles, NOT thatched, no straw, no hay, no pointed gables.
No characters, no people, no animals, no text, no letters, no UI, no fog, no sun glow.

Low-resolution pixel art map for a top-down 3/4 view RPG, exactly 16 pixels per map tile, a grid of 32 by 24 tiles filling the whole frame edge to edge with no border.
The first reference image is the layout at the final size: trace over it exactly. Sand-coloured areas are walkable ground and must stay plain flat light earth with exactly the same outline; white squares and grey squares (where people and gates will be) are plain ground too. Brown squares are small objects drawn on that spot. Dark green areas are blocked and must be filled with rock, cliff, pines or dry grass, never with path. Do not add, widen, move or remove any path, and draw nothing on the paths. The path at the top right leaves the map through the top edge; every other path ends at the rocks.
The second reference image is only a small sample of the pixel style of the same mountain pass (colours, 1px outlines, rock, cliff and roof shapes); it is not a layout, do not copy its hut or paths.
Place: the summit of a mountain pass at dusk, every colour muted to about half its saturation, warm low light from the upper left. The lower half is the summit yard, a wide flat earth clearing. In the middle of the yard, on the blocked block just below the middle, stands the landmark: a small open wooden rest shelter whose straight plank roof is half broken, the left half of the roof still on, the right half snapped and sagging down at an angle with a few missing planks, standing on four square wooden posts with a low bench inside; the brown square at its right corner is one of its sturdy wooden posts. The brown square at the upper left of the yard is a round iron brazier on a ring of stones. The brown square at the lower right corner of the yard is a wooden direction post with three blank arrow boards pointing different ways. Brown squares with bundles of pine twigs tied with straw rope: one at the foot of a big grey rock on the far left (west), one on the very edge of the cliff on the right (east), one in the lower left corner of the yard. Right of the yard the ground ends in a sheer cliff edge falling away; left of the yard big grey boulders. The upper half is a wide, bare, open summit ridge of flat earth surrounded by rocky blocked ground; between the yard and the ridge runs a band of broken grey rock with a narrow gap in the middle. A narrow path at the top right climbs off the map.
Style: crisp hard-edged pixels with no blur and no anti-aliasing, limited palette, 1px darker-hue outlines on objects, trees and rocks (never black), flat ground shading with 2-3 shades, soft cel shading, child-friendly, light from the upper left. Paths are plain flat light earth, calm, with clean edges.
No characters, no people, no animals, no text, no letters, no UI, no fog, no mist, no sun, no sun rays, no glow, no sparkles, no fire flames.

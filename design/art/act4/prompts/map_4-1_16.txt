Low-resolution pixel art map for a top-down 3/4 view RPG, exactly 16 pixels per map tile, a grid of 32 by 24 tiles filling the whole frame edge to edge with no border.
The first reference image is the layout at the final size: trace over it exactly. Sand-coloured areas are walkable paths and must stay plain flat light earth path with exactly the same outline; white squares and grey squares (where people will stand) are plain path too. Brown squares are small objects drawn on that spot. Dark green areas are blocked and must be filled with mountainside, never with path. Do not add, widen, move or remove any path, and draw nothing on the paths. The path at the top left leaves the map through the top edge; every other path ends at the rocks.
The second reference image is only a small sample of the pixel style of the same world (colours, 1px outlines, tree and roof shapes); it is not a layout, do not copy its house or paths.
Place: a steep mountain pass road at dusk, the sunset just beginning: warm low light from the upper left, long cool shadows, every colour muted to about half its saturation (dusty olive greens, grey-brown rock, soft rose and amber highlights). The sand road zigzags up the mountainside from the lower right to the top in long straight runs joined by sharp hairpin bends; curves have turned into straight lines. Each horizontal run is a ledge cut into the slope: right below the lower edge of every run is a short cliff face of layered grey-brown rock, and between the runs the blocked ground is steep rocky hillside with short dry grass, scattered boulders, a few dark pine trees and low shrubs. Brown squares: at the left middle a small iron brazier with a kettle on it, and on the blocked ground right next to it, filling the space between the two left runs, a small roadside tea house: a low wooden hut with a straight slanted plank roof, an open front with a wooden counter, two stools and a cloth hanging at the door; at the lower middle a small cairn of stacked round stones at the roadside; on the right, above the middle run, a wooden signpost leaning crookedly to one side with a blank board; at the top middle, a pine tree with its trunk snapped and the crown bent down. Near the top the road climbs to a small landing and goes off the top edge towards the summit.
Style: crisp hard-edged pixels with no blur and no anti-aliasing, limited palette, 1px darker-hue outlines on objects, trees and rocks (never black), flat ground shading with 2-3 shades, soft cel shading, child-friendly, light from the upper left. Paths are plain flat light earth, calm, with clean edges.
No characters, no people, no animals, no text, no letters, no UI, no fog, no mist, no sun, no sun rays, no glow, no sparkles, no fire flames.

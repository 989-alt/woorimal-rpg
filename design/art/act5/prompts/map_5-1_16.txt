Low-resolution pixel art map for a top-down 3/4 view RPG, exactly 16 pixels per map tile, a grid of 32 by 24 tiles filling the whole frame edge to edge with no border.
The first reference image is the layout at the final size: trace over it exactly. Sand-coloured areas are walkable ground and must stay plain flat paving with exactly the same outline; white squares and grey squares (where people will stand) are plain paving too. Brown squares are small objects drawn on that spot. Dark green areas are blocked and must be filled with walls, stone blocks and dead garden beds, never with paving. Do not add, widen, move or remove any path, and draw nothing on the paths. The path at the top middle leaves the map through the top edge; every other path ends at a wall.
The second reference image is only a small sample of the pixel style of the same world (1px outlines, roof and stone shapes); it is not a layout and not a colour sample, do not copy its house, trees or paths.

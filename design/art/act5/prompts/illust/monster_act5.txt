Each figure is a separate soft body with small, soft, rounded heads made of fuzzy mist that blend into the neck (not hard balls), narrow drooping shoulders and limp arms hanging down; all the heads are bowed low, faceless (no eyes, no mouth, no features at all). The figures lower down are darker and more melted together, and at the very bottom the whole crowd pours down into soft trailing strands of mist. Sad, tired and lonely rather than scary, like a crowd of forgotten words waiting for someone to call their names.

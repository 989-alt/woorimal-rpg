16px pixel art map for a top-down 3/4 view RPG, a cozy fantasy village in the early morning. Crisp hard-edged pixels on a 16-pixel tile grid, limited palette of about 30 colours, 1px darker-hue outlines on objects (never black), flat ground shading with 2-3 shades and no outline on the ground, light from the upper left.
Content: rounded puffy roofs in warm ochre and slate blue with arched doors and round-topped windows; the landmark is a round-roofed cottage with chimney smoke on the left side; large clay storage jars beside it and a clothesline with laundry; round dark-green tree canopies around the edges; a teal stream with a darker teal outline curving through the bottom right with a small arched wooden bridge; sandy light paths at least two tiles wide connecting the houses; grass ledges; a few flowers.
At the north edge one narrow alley and the laundry there are desaturated grey with thin dithered grey fog creeping in.
No characters, no text, no UI.

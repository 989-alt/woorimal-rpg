Low-resolution pixel art map for a top-down 3/4 view RPG, exactly 16 pixels per map tile, a grid of 32 by 24 tiles filling the whole frame edge to edge with no border.
The first reference image is the layout at the final size: trace over it exactly. Sand-coloured areas are walkable paths and must stay plain flat sand with exactly the same outline; white squares (where people will stand) and grey squares are plain sand path too. Brown squares are small objects drawn on that spot. Dark green areas are blocked: fill them with water, banks, reeds, rocks and trees as described below, never with sand. Do not add, widen, move or remove any path, and draw nothing on the paths. The path at the top right leaves the map at the right edge.
The second reference image is only a small sample of the pixel style of the same world (colours, 1px outlines, tree, rock and water shapes); it is not a layout, do not copy its house or paths.
Place: a creek in the late afternoon, all about the colour of water. A wide creek crosses the whole map from left to right through the blocked band between the two long sand banks (rows 9 to 12). The narrow vertical path in the middle of that band is a small arched wooden footbridge over the creek; its deck is pale sand-coloured planks with thin brown rails on both sides. The small sand area on the right inside the creek is a big flat fishing rock, with a round stepping stone below it. The upper part of the creek and the small pond at the top of the map are pale and faded, grey-blue, still and misty; the rest of the creek is clear blue water with only a few tiny single white pixel dots as glints, no gold, no glow. Brown squares: on the upper bank a small clay bowl on the left end and one on the right end; on the lower bank a clump of tall reeds at the water's edge on the left; at the lower left a wooden mooring post at a small ferry landing; at the lower right a small clay bowl beside a big flat washing rock. The top path runs around the misty pond and then along a narrow upper stream to the right edge. All other blocked areas are grassy banks, round trees and bushes. No houses.
Style: crisp hard-edged pixels with no blur and no anti-aliasing, limited palette, 1px darker-hue outlines on objects and trees (never black), flat ground shading with 2-3 shades, soft cel shading, child-friendly, light from the upper left. Paths are plain flat bright sand, calm, with clean edges.
No characters, no people, no animals, no boats, no text, no letters, no UI, no sun rays, no glow, no sparkles.

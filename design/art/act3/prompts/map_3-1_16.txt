Low-resolution pixel art map for a top-down 3/4 view RPG, exactly 16 pixels per map tile, a grid of 32 by 24 tiles filling the whole frame edge to edge with no border.
The first reference image is the layout at the final size: trace over it exactly. Sand-coloured areas are walkable paths and must stay plain flat light earth path with exactly the same outline; white squares (where people will stand) are plain path too. Brown squares are small objects drawn on that spot. Dark green areas are blocked and must be filled with dense round trees, bushes and undergrowth, never with path. Do not add, widen, move or remove any path, and draw nothing on the paths. The path at the top right leaves the map at the right edge; every other path ends at the trees.
The second reference image is only a small sample of the pixel style of the same world (colours, 1px outlines, tree shapes); it is not a layout, do not copy its house, river or paths.

No houses, no fences, no buildings anywhere, only forest. A few flat patches of brighter leaf-green sunlight lie on the green ground under the trees: plain flat light-green shapes with hard edges, not yellow, not gold, no glow.
